How to play

  • Steer with arrows / WASD, or drag on touch. Your gun auto-fires.
  • Destroy enemy vehicles for points. Clear the quota to face the level boss.
  • Catch power-ups:
  • WSpread TTriple RRapid SShield NNitro +Life BBomb
